# Economics

## Phase 1

No pricing or savings model is implemented yet.

## Planned Model

- Current monthly spend: sum of entered tool spend.
- Optimized monthly spend: current spend minus deterministic savings.
- Annual savings: monthly savings multiplied by 12.
- Credex CTA threshold: savings greater than `$500` per month.
- Efficient-stack message threshold: savings lower than `$100` per month.
